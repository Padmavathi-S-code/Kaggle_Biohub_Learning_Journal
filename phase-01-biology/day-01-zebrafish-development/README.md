## 🧬 Why This Matters for BioHub

BioHub focuses on detecting and tracking cells through **3D space and time** during zebrafish development.

Understanding the biology first helps connect the computational tasks to the underlying biological processes:

```text
Cell division
      ↓
New daughter cells

Cell migration
      ↓
Cells change position

Development
      ↓
Cell identities and structures change

3D microscopy + time
      ↓
Detect cells
      ↓
Track cells
      ↓
Identify divisions
      ↓
Reconstruct cell lineage
