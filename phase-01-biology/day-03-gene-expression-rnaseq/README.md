## 🧬 Connection to BioHub

BioHub involves analysing cells across space and time.

During development, cells can:

- Divide and produce daughter cells
- Move through tissues
- Change their appearance
- Differentiate into specialised cells
- Disappear or become difficult to track

Therefore, cell tracking is not simply about locating cells. It also involves understanding their **identity, movement, division, and lineage** over time.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

**Cell lineage:** The developmental history of a cell and its descendants.

**Cell fate:** The developmental outcome a cell is expected to follow.

**Differentiation:** The process through which cells acquire specialised characteristics.

**Parent-daughter relationship:** The relationship between a cell and the cells produced through its division.
