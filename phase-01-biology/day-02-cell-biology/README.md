**Phase:** 01 — Biology & Problem Understanding

**Topic:** Cell Biology

## 🎯 Learning Goal

Understand the basic biology of individual cells, including the cell cycle, division, migration, differentiation, apoptosis, and lineage.

## 📚 Topics Covered

- Cell structure
- Cell cycle
- G1, S, G2 and M phases
- Mitosis
- Cytokinesis
- Cell proliferation
- Cell migration
- Cell differentiation
- Apoptosis
- Cell lineage

## 🧬 Connection to BioHub

BioHub requires cells to be detected and followed through 3D space and time.

The biological events that make this challenging include:

- Cells divide
- Cells move
- Cells change during development
- Cells can disappear
- Daughter cells inherit relationships from parent cells
