# Biomedical_AI_Learning_Journal

Documenting my journey of trying to learn biomedical AI from the ground up through structured study, practical projects, research papers, and practice building similar projects like the Kaggle BioHub Cell Tracking During Development of zebrafish competition for various datasets and species.
Also replicate and learn from competitions like Stanford RNA 3D Folding: Great for learning deep learning applied to structural biology and 3D spatial geometry of biological molecules and Cell Tracking Challenge (CTC): An external, long-running community benchmark (celltrackingchallenge.net) dedicated explicitly to evaluating 2D/3D cell segmentation and tracking algorithms.
